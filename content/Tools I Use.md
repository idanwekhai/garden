## Podcasting
- Adobe Audition: Editing, Mixing, Solo/offline recording
- Spotify for creators (formerly Anchor): Hosting, Distribution
- Podcastle: Online interview and recording
- Pocket Casts or Antenna Pod: Aggregating and listening

- Google Keep: Idea capture, Poems
- Google docs: Editing, Academic Writing
