I've been trying to note how certain vocations use a different terminology for the people they provide service. All of these are people who are served, but the word changes based on the vocation.

| Vocation            | Refer to people as... | Example                                       |
| ------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Salesperson         | Account               | I have a close relationship with that account |
| Health Practitioner | Patient               | The patient will come in today                |
| Consultant          | Client                | I met up with the client today                |
| Waiter/Waitress     | Party                 | We're expecting a party of 2 at noon          |
| Teacher             | Students              | My students are here now                      |
| Lawyer              | Client                | They're a high profile client of mine         |
| Therapist           | Client/Patient        | They're a vulnerable client                   |
| Tech Support        | User                  | The user didn't click a button                |
| Project Manager     | Stakeholder           | We'll present to the stakeholders             |
| Support Agent       | Customer              | The customer wasn't happy                     |
| Marketer            | Audience              | The audience didn't like it too much          |
