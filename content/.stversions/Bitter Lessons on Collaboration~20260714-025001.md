Some lessons learned from collaborating with individuals or teams.

- Smaller teams are always better. If the team is lacking in creativity or output, a bigger team might not necessarily solve the problem.
- Absolutely no rockstars! If they're bigger than the team, they shouldn't be part of the team.
- Be ready to walk alone. It doesn't matter who told you they'll walk with you, keep walking.
- Their initial excitement about the project means absolutely nothing! 
- People over promise! Don't get exited about what people say until the work is done.
- Aligned incentives matter way more than you think.
- A bad communicator will injure you in countless unpredictable ways.
- It's a big problem if they don't share in your excitement and urgency. Don't ignore it!
- If they can't think/brainstorm with you, they shouldn't be working with you.
